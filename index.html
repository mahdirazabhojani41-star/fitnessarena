MY STORY

My Fitness Journey

In 2024, I was 86 kg and I started noticing that I was gaining a lot of body fat.

I decided to make a serious change in my lifestyle. I started going to the gym and followed a strict diet along with my training.

I avoided added sugar, unnecessary oil, bakery items and junk food. I focused on eating clean and staying consistent.

For training, I followed a Push-Pull-Legs workout routine along with cardio.

The journey was not about changing in one day. It was about following my routine, controlling my diet and staying consistent.

This journey inspired me to create FitArena, a platform that combines training, nutrition, goals, challenges and progress in one place.
